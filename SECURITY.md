# Reporting a security problem

Thank you for looking. An archive meant to last centuries needs people
who poke at it early.

## How to tell us

Use this repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**.
That opens a private channel visible only to the maintainer.

**Please do not open a public issue for a security problem**, and
please do not email: The Human Record deliberately receives no mail.
The domain publishes a null MX and refuses inbound messages outright —
so a message sent there is not read, it is rejected. That is the one
channel, and it is private.

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you think it
lets someone do. A rough note beats a polished one that never gets
sent.

## What we will do

We will read it, tell you what we think, and fix what needs fixing. If
you would like credit we will give it; if you would rather stay
anonymous, that is fine too.

We are one steward, not a company with a rota, so we cannot promise an
answer within hours. We can promise that a real person reads every
report and that nothing gets quietly ignored.

## What matters most here

Ordered by what would actually harm someone, rather than by what scores
highest on a severity table:

1. **Anything that exposes a testimony an author sealed**, or links an
   author to words they chose to keep private.
2. **Anything that alters a record, or makes an alteration
   undetectable.** The whole institution rests on `verify.py` being
   able to catch changes.
3. **Anything that spends a registry number without a human at the
   desk approving it.** Numbers are permanent and cannot be reused.
4. **Anything that defeats one-human-one-record**, or reveals the
   private uniqueness ledger.
5. **Anything that takes the archive offline**, or runs up costs until
   it goes offline.

## Things that are already true, and are not findings

These are deliberate, documented, and not bugs:

- **The code is public**, including the anti-abuse gate. The archive's
  safety does not rest on anyone failing to read it — it rests on
  secrets that are not here, on keys with 128 bits of entropy, and on
  a human reading every submission before any number is spent.
- **The gate's difficulty and its minimum writing time are published**
  by the API itself. The writer's browser needs them.
- **A lost continuity key cannot be recovered.** The institution has
  never held one. This is the design, not an oversight.
- **Testimonies are absent from this repository.** They are meant to
  be. The log carries fingerprints only.

## Out of scope

Reports generated by a scanner with no evidence of real impact,
findings that require the operator's own machine or accounts to be
compromised first, and issues in Cloudflare or GitHub rather than in
this archive.
